Practice help

  • Tap نمایش پاسخ to reveal the answer, then جملهٔ بعدی to continue.
  • Tap a Persian sentence to jump to it. In Auto, press and hold the sentence to jump.
  • Outside the answer, tap the lesson background to advance, or swipe right for next and left for previous.
  • Inside the answer, scroll, select text, or tap a heading to open its notes.
  • Use ⋯ → Read aloud to choose Persian, English, or both. The speaker button mutes and restores your selection.
  • Use ☰ to switch exercises. Back closes an open panel.
