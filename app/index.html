Auto mode

The countdown waits while the answer is being read aloud, while a panel like this one is open, and while you are typing in the typing box, so its time is always quiet time. Any move (next, previous, skip, a click on a sentence) starts the time of the new step afresh. “pause” keeps the step and its time; “play” goes on.

What you can say

Say each one on its own, with a short pause before and after it. Inside a sentence it is ignored, so the English answers you say aloud do not count. The mic does not listen while an answer is being read aloud. The toast at the bottom left shows what was heard: a word with “?” was not sure enough and nothing happened. Close this list with Esc, a click, G, or by saying “commands”.
