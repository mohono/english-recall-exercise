Settings

The countdown waits while the answer is being read aloud, while a panel like this one is open, and while you are typing in the typing box, so its time is always quiet time. Any move (next, previous, a click on a sentence) starts the time of the new step afresh. Pause (P) keeps the step and its time; P again goes on. The typing box can be turned off here; when it is on, it never takes the keyboard by itself.
